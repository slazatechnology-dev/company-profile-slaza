# DESIGN.md — PT. SLAZA TECHNOLOGY MANDIRI

Digital Product & Industrial IoT Engineering Studio

## 1. Brand Identity & Personality

- **Company:** PT. Slaza Technology Mandiri
- **Core Competency:** High-Performance Software Engineering, Custom Cloud Backends, and Industrial IoT / Embedded Systems.
- **Personality:** Clean, editorial, authoritative, transparent, and grounded in real-world engineering.
- **Aesthetic Benchmark:** High-end digital product studios (Work & Co, MetaLab, Panic) — typography-first, expansive white space, structured grids, zero decorative fluff.

## 2. Dials (Anti-Slop Liveliness Settings)

- **ENERGY: 2 (Balanced)** — Bold typography and clear visual contrast without visual screaming or neon gimmicks.
- **RHYTHM: 2 (Structured)** — Editorial pacing; clean alternating layouts between hero narrative, deep-dive case study, capability breakdown, and direct consultation.
- **Dial:** ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1
- **MOTION: 1 (Calm)** — Intentional micro-interactions only (smooth button hovers, subtle card border highlights, clean navigation transition). Zero continuous looping animations, zero pulsing status dots, zero simulated sensor graphs.

## 3. Color System (Strict Palette Restraint)

- **Canvas Base:** Pure Studio Whiteacce (`#ffffff`) and Soft Architectural Off-White (`#f8fafc`).
- **Surface & Containers:** Clean Crisp Cards (`#ffffff`) wit1h subtle structural borders (`#e2e8f0` / `#cbd5e1`).
- **Typography Colors:**
  - Headings & Primary Text: Pitch Charcoal (`#09090b`) — high readability, crisp contrast (15:1+ against white).
  - Body Text: Deep Slate (`#334155`).
  - Metadata / Subtitles: Muted Neutral (`#64748b`).
- **Single Accent:** Royal Cobalt (`#1d4ed8`) for primary actions, active link states, and focused interactions.
- **Accent phrase in headings (identity motif, owner decision 2026-11-11, referensi gits.id):** the hero headline and every section title color one key phrase in Royal Cobalt (`AccentText.vue`, `.accent-text`). One phrase per heading, never body text, never a second accent color.
- **Hero visual:** a real product screenshot (yaytopup.id) on the right. No stock photos, 3D renders, or illustrations until real photos of hardware, team, or office exist.
- **Dark Neutral Accents:** Deep Navy/Black (`#0f172a`) for select high-contrast callouts or footer base.

## 4. Typography Rules

- **Display & Body Font:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` (-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto, sans-serif).
- **Scale Contrast:** Large, confident headlines with tight tracking (`-0.03em`), generous line-height on paragraphs (`1.65`), and clear hierarchical separation.
- **Monospace Font:** `JetBrains Mono` reserved strictly for factual technical data (hardware specs, API payloads, port numbers). Never used for decorative headings or `//` prefixed labels.

## 5. Copywriting Standards (Zero AI Slop)

- No em dashes (`—`).
- No generic buzzwords ("Cutting-edge", "Revolutionary", "Next-Gen", "Seamless", "AI Powered").
- Plain, direct, human Indonesian and English copy explaining actual problems solved and real engineering capabilities delivered.
- Direct contact pathways: Clear consultation form, official email, and direct WhatsApp channel.
